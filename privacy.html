Privacy policy

Aurelias Studio · last updated 23 November 2026

What it reads

When the owner of a YouTube channel connects it, Aurelias Studio reads that channel's YouTube Analytics and YouTube Reporting data with read-only access (the scopes yt-analytics.readonly and youtube.readonly): numbers such as views, watch time, audience retention, traffic sources, impressions and click-through rate of the channel's own videos, and the titles of those videos.

What it does with it

It shows these numbers to the channel's own team, and uses them to suggest topics and improve scripts for that channel. It does not upload, change or delete anything on YouTube.

Where it is kept

The data and the access token are stored only on the one computer where Aurelias Studio runs. They are not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. The text of scripts may be sent to AI services (Anthropic, OpenAI) to write and review them; YouTube Analytics data is only included where needed to advise on the channel's own videos.

Removing access

The channel's owner can withdraw access at any time in their Google account under Security, Third-party apps with account access, or by pressing "Disconnect" in Aurelias Studio, which deletes the stored token.

Contact

Through the email address shown on Google's consent screen when connecting.
