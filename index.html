Aurelias Studio

A private tool for making documentary videos for a few YouTube channels.

Aurelias Studio runs on one computer and is used by a small team of three people. It helps them research topics, write and fact-check scripts, design thumbnails and follow how their own videos do on YouTube.

To show how their own channel's videos perform, it reads that channel's YouTube Analytics with read-only permission, given by the channel's owner. It does not upload, change or delete anything on YouTube, and it is not offered to anyone else.
