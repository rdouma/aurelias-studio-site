Who may use it

Aurelias Studio is a private tool. It is used only by its own small team for their own YouTube channels and is not offered, sold or provided to anyone else.

What connecting allows

By connecting a YouTube channel, its owner allows Aurelias Studio to read that channel's YouTube Analytics and Reporting data, read-only, as described in the privacy policy. The tool never posts, changes or deletes anything on YouTube.

Ending it

The owner can stop at any time by pressing "Disconnect" in Aurelias Studio or by removing its access in their Google account. Use of YouTube data also falls under the YouTube Terms of Service and Google's privacy policy.

No warranty

Aurelias Studio is provided as it is, for the team's own use, without any warranty.
